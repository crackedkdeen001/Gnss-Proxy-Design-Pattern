==============================================================================
  TABLE 4.2  Effect of validity window length on activations and staleness
==============================================================================
    Window (s)  Activations   Energy (mJ)   Max staleness (s)
             0           20          5000                 0.0
            30           20          5000                 0.0
            60           10          2500                30.0
           120            5          1250                95.9
           300            2           500               270.4
           600            1           250               570.7

  A window of zero disables caching and reproduces direct access. Longer
  windows reduce activations monotonically and increase maximum position
  age correspondingly. The appropriate value is application dependent.

==============================================================================
  4.3  Behaviour when acquisition fails
==============================================================================
  Fix obtained under clear sky     (  6.51559,   3.38945) acquired 17:08:40.974
  Signal obstructed, fix returned  (  6.51559,   3.38945) acquired 17:08:40.974 [STALE]

  Stale fallbacks served           1
  The last known position is returned marked stale rather than failing
  outright. The stored timestamp is not refreshed, so the receiver is
  retried on the next request instead of the fix appearing valid again.

==============================================================================
  4.4  Concurrent requests
==============================================================================
  Concurrent requests issued       20
  Receiver activations             1
  Energy consumed (mJ)             250

  Requests arriving during an acquisition are coalesced onto it rather
  than each triggering a separate activation.
